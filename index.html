  • Each concern can earn up to 6 points from Questions 1–5. Question 4 counts double for People because it’s the only People question.
  • A Question 6 pick is treated like a “C” answer (2 points), then weighted: 2x adds 4 points, 3x adds 6.
  • If the Question 6 concern ties with another, the Question 6 concern wins. Other ties follow the order Risk, Cost, People, Time.
  • “Your Plan Looks Healthy” shows only when Question 6 is “None of these” and every answer to Questions 1–5 is an A or B. Otherwise the highest-scoring concern wins.
